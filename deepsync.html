DeepSync

Guided breathing. Personal sleep and recovery insights.

DeepSync is an iOS wellness application in development that combines guided breathing with insights from supported Apple Health and Apple Watch measurements. It helps users explore their personal patterns in sleep, heart rate variability, and recovery, while making data sources and measurement limitations clear.

HRV Insights

Explore HRV trends with clear measurement context and source information.

Guided Breathing

Practice paced breathing and review your physiological response when compatible measurements are available.

Sleep & Recovery

Review sleep and recovery patterns alongside your breathing history. Associations do not establish cause and effect.
