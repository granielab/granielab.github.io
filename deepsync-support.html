Onboarding 1

How DeepSync works

DeepSync connects four steps: a breathing plan, an observed physiological response, later sleep or recovery outcomes when available, and comparison across repeated sessions. Its Digital Twin is a personal evidence summary—not a clinical model and not a guarantee of benefit.

Before

Your context and plan

DeepSync considers the session goal, timing, available history, and current evidence when presenting a breathing plan.

During

Your breathing session

The app records the planned cadence and the measurements that compatible Apple devices make available.

After

Response and outcomes

DeepSync summarizes the immediate response and, when possible, links a pre-sleep session with the following night.

Over time

Compare and learn

The app separates descriptive averages from forecasts and later matched outcomes. More samples increase context but do not automatically establish causation.

Onboarding 2

Why breathing may relate to sleep

Slow, comfortable breathing can support a calmer pre-sleep routine by reducing behavioral arousal and encouraging attention away from the day. Breathing also changes normal beat-to-beat heart-rate patterns through respiratory sinus arrhythmia. These immediate effects do not prove that a particular cadence will improve sleep stages that night.

For a consistent pre-sleep session

  • Complete the session at a similar point in your wind-down routine.
  • Use the recommended cadence only while it remains comfortable; never strain or hold your breath unless the session explicitly calls for it.
  • Stay seated or reclined and minimize movement during measurement windows.
  • Wear your compatible device as instructed and allow time for data to reach Apple Health.
  • Record relevant context honestly, including unusual exercise, alcohol, illness, travel, caffeine, or schedule changes when the app asks.
Interpretation: Estimated Deep, REM, Core, and Awake stages are wearable-derived estimates. A higher value is not automatically better, and breathing-session associations do not establish cause and effect.

Onboarding 3

What HRV is—and why it matters

Heart rate variability, or HRV, describes variation in time between successive heartbeats. It is not the same as heart rate. HRV can change with breathing, posture, sleep, training, stress, illness, alcohol, measurement timing, and sensor conditions.

DeepSync uses HRV as a contextual wellness signal. A single high or low measurement is rarely meaningful by itself. Your own repeated measurements under similar conditions are usually more informative than comparison with another person.

Important measurement distinctions

  • Breathing-session RMSSD summarizes short-term beat-to-beat variation during a defined session.
  • Wake HRV is a morning measurement attributed to the wake context when timing and data-quality rules are met.
  • Apple overnight HRV may use SDNN and must not be treated as interchangeable with session RMSSD.
  • Unavailable means DeepSync cannot support the value from eligible data; it does not mean your HRV was zero.

Onboarding 4

How to measure morning recovery

  1. After waking, remain relaxed and avoid exercise, caffeine, or a stressful task before the measurement when practical.
  2. Aim to begin the Wake HRV session about 10 minutes after your resolved wake time.
  3. Use a similar posture each morning, keep your arm supported, and minimize movement and talking.
  4. Follow the session instructions and let DeepSync finish checking Apple Health.
  5. If the result remains unavailable, tap the available processing or Health refresh control once; repeated taps do not create missing sensor data.

The Recovery Snapshot combines available domains and shows limitations when important information is missing. It should inform reflection—not decide whether it is medically safe to exercise.

Onboarding 5

How to support healthy sleep and Deep-sleep opportunity

No app can guarantee more Deep sleep, and consumer wearables estimate sleep stages rather than measuring them like a clinical sleep study. Focus first on behaviors that support adequate, regular, uninterrupted sleep:

  • Keep a reasonably consistent sleep and wake schedule.
  • Allow enough time in bed for your sleep need.
  • Get daytime activity and morning light when appropriate for you.
  • Create a dark, quiet, and comfortably cool sleep environment.
  • Limit late caffeine and consider how alcohol affects continuity and awakenings.
  • Use pre-sleep breathing as a repeatable wind-down practice—not as a guaranteed stage-changing treatment.
  • Review trends across multiple nights and consider Awake time, WASO, total sleep, and how you feel—not Deep sleep alone.

Persistent insomnia, loud snoring, breathing pauses, severe daytime sleepiness, chest symptoms, or other concerning symptoms deserve professional evaluation.

Help

Common questions

Why is a value unavailable?

The compatible device may not have been worn, Apple Health permission may be off, the record may not have arrived yet, or the available measurement may not meet DeepSync's timing, attribution, or data-quality requirements. Unavailable values are not replaced with estimates.

Why does DeepSync differ from another app?

Apps may select different source records, sleep episodes, stage definitions, windows, or update times. DeepSync keeps source provenance and does not combine sleep stages from different source timelines.

What if I miss a breathing session?

Continue with the next appropriate session. Missing a night or morning does not require deleting your history. DeepSync should show that evidence is missing rather than inventing a value.

How do I refresh Health data?

Open the relevant tab or use the available Update Health or processing control. Apple Health delivery can be delayed; keep the devices nearby and allow syncing to complete before trying again.

How should I report a problem?

In DeepSync Settings, create a Support Package. Review the package before sharing it. Include the approximate date, time, tab, action, and what you expected to happen. Do not send a complete personal export unless support specifically requests it and you choose to share it.
