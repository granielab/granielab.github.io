DeepSync

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeepSync, an iPhone wellness application and its BreathPacer Apple Watch companion, handles information. DeepSync is operated by Graniela Engineering & Analytics, LLC of St. Petersburg, Florida, USA ("Graniela Engineering," "we," or "us").

1. DeepSync is a local-first wellness application

DeepSync provides guided breathing and personal insights based on information available on your devices. DeepSync does not require a DeepSync account. The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or an automatic service that uploads your health history to Graniela Engineering.

DeepSync is intended for general wellness and personal information. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

2. Information processed by DeepSync

When you grant permission, DeepSync can read selected information from Apple Health, including heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, sleep records, mindfulness sessions, workouts, and available heartbeat series. Records can include timestamps and the source app or device. Availability depends on your permissions, compatible devices, and the information present in Apple Health.

DeepSync also processes information you provide or create in the app, such as breathing-session settings and history, context selections, notes, subjective feedback, preferences, diagnostic settings, and locally calculated insights, recommendations, and evidence summaries. Optional research exports can include profile categories you choose to provide and a locally generated participant identifier.

Health records made available to Apple Health by another device or application, including Oura, may be available to DeepSync through Apple Health. DeepSync does not require an Oura account and does not connect directly to Oura's service.

3. How information is used

DeepSync uses this information to conduct guided breathing sessions; display sleep, HRV, recovery, and breathing-response information; maintain your session history; calculate personal baselines and insights; provide reminders you enable; troubleshoot app operation; and create exports you request. The iPhone and paired Apple Watch exchange the session configuration, progress, measurements, and results needed to conduct a session.

Health information is not used for advertising, data brokerage, credit decisions, or sale to third parties. DeepSync Plus purchases do not require you to grant additional health-data permissions.

4. Storage and information shared outside the app

DeepSync stores its working information locally in the app's container and device preferences. Some DeepSync or BreathPacer sessions may create Health records, such as workouts, when you authorize that behavior. Your device, Apple Health, iCloud backup settings, and Apple account are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

DeepSync does not automatically transmit your personal health history to Graniela Engineering. Information leaves the app only when you initiate or authorize an action, such as exporting data, creating a research report, creating a support package, sharing a file, or using Apple's purchase services.

  • Exports: Personal exports can contain detailed health records and timestamps. Research exports reduce identifying details but are not guaranteed to be anonymous. You choose whether and where to share an export.
  • Support packages: A support package can contain app identity, operational logs, errors, record counts, and selected session or health-processing details. Review it and share it only when you want support.
  • Purchases: Apple processes DeepSync Plus subscriptions and supplies DeepSync with signed entitlement information. DeepSync does not receive your full payment-card details.

5. Third parties

DeepSync relies on Apple platform services such as Apple Health, StoreKit, iCloud or device backup when enabled by you, and iPhone–Apple Watch communication. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and email support is handled by the provider of the email service you use and our receiving email provider. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and security practices.

DeepSync does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If that changes, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the new processing is introduced. Service providers receiving information from us must protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

App information remains on your device until you delete records using available app controls or delete the app, subject to copies that may remain in device or iCloud backups. Revoking Apple Health permission prevents future access but does not automatically delete information DeepSync previously stored. Deleting DeepSync does not delete separate records in Apple Health or files you exported; manage those copies using Apple Health, Files, backup, and recipient controls.

Information you voluntarily send to Graniela Engineering for support is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate and resolve the request. Support packages and their working copies are normally deleted within 30 days after the support case is closed, unless a longer period is required by law, needed to address an active security or legal issue, or requested by you. De-identified issue summaries may be retained to improve reliability.

7. Your choices and consent

You control Apple Health access in iPhone Settings and the Health app. You can disable DeepSync reminders in DeepSync Settings or iOS notification settings. You can choose not to enter optional notes or profile information, not to create or share exports, and not to purchase DeepSync Plus.

For information you have sent directly to Graniela Engineering, you may ask what we retain, request correction or deletion, or withdraw consent by contacting us. We may need to verify the request and retain limited information when legally required. Graniela Engineering cannot remotely access or erase information that remains only on your device.

8. Children

DeepSync is not directed to children under 13, and Graniela Engineering does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through DeepSync. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information directly to us.

9. Security and policy changes

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a local-first application. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect exported files as sensitive information.

We may update this policy when DeepSync's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL with its effective date. Material changes will be described through the app, website, or release information as appropriate.
